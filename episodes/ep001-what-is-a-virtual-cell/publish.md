**Title**
What Is a Virtual Cell? We Never Touched A — Yet A Doubled | Virtual Cell Ep. 1

**Description**
What does a "virtual cell" actually try to predict? In this first episode we build a toy cell with just three genes, switch one off, and watch a gene we never touched double. That surprise is the whole idea: a cell's current state isn't enough to predict what happens next. You also need the rules inside.

A virtual cell takes a cell's state and what you do to it, and predicts what the cell turns into.
