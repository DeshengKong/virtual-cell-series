# 试音文本（约 30–40 秒）

故意包含易读错的术语：CRISPR、Perturb-seq、scGPT、UMAP。

## 中文

如果有一天，医生能先在电脑里给你的细胞试药——哪种药有效，哪种有副作用，不用在你身上试，屏幕上就能看到。这就是"虚拟细胞"想做的事。它的本质，其实只有一句话：告诉电脑细胞现在是什么状态，再告诉它你对细胞做了什么，让它预测细胞接下来会变成什么。比如，用 CRISPR 敲掉一个基因，用 Perturb-seq 测出几千个基因的变化，再交给 scGPT 这样的模型去学习规律，最后把结果画成一张 UMAP 图。

## English

Imagine a doctor could test a drug on your cells inside a computer first — which drug works, which one causes side effects — all on a screen, without trying anything on you. That is what a "virtual cell" aims to do. At its core, the idea fits in one sentence: tell the computer what state a cell is in now, tell it what you did to the cell, and let it predict what the cell becomes next. For example, knock out a gene with CRISPR, measure how thousands of genes respond with Perturb-seq, let a model like scGPT learn the pattern, and finally draw the result as a UMAP plot.
